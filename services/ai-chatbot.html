Foundations

What is an Enterprise AI Chatbot?

Unlike rigid rules-based chat scripts that frustrate visitors with menu trees, modern AI chatbots utilize advanced Natural Language Understanding (NLU) trained directly on your organization's verified knowledge bases.

The assistant grasps colloquial questions, recognizes customer intent, answers complex multi-part queries, and executes structured tasks like booking meetings and updating customer records in real time.
