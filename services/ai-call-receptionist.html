Voice Architecture

What is an AI Call Receptionist?

An AI call receptionist is an automated, voice-driven telephony system capable of understanding spoken language in real time, conducting intelligent two-way dialogue, and completing tasks just like a human front-desk coordinator.

By pairing low-latency Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) with specialized conversational language models, your callers experience instantaneous answers without listening to endless touch-tone menu trees.

Key Voice Receptionist Capabilities

  • Instant Zero-Wait Pickup: Every caller is greeted on the first ring, 24/7/365.
  • Real-Time Calendar Booking: Integrates directly with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Acuity.
  • Emergency & VIP Call Routing: Escalates high-priority callers immediately to staff cell phones.
  • Automated Audio Summaries & Transcripts: Audio recordings and written briefs sent via email & CRM.
