Privacy Policy

Last revised: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Haash Corporation website or submit an inquiry through our contact form, we collect personal information that you provide voluntarily. This includes your full name, business email address, company name, telephone or WhatsApp contact number, service interests, project timeline, and technical requirements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected information solely to:

  • Review and respond to your business inquiries and service requests.
  • Prepare accurate technical proposals, scopes of work, and project estimates.
  • Communicate operational updates regarding current and prospective engagements.
  • Maintain security and prevent malicious contact form spam or abuse.

3. Data Protection & Transmission

We enforce HTTPS encryption for all web communications. Contact inquiries are transmitted securely to verified internal email inboxes using trusted, industry-standard transactional delivery providers (such as Resend). Our website includes links to third-party services including WhatsApp (Meta Platforms), Facebook, and Instagram. When you click these external links or interact with WhatsApp chat buttons, your data is governed by the respective privacy practices of those independent providers.

You retain the right to inspect, correct, or request the permanent deletion of any personal data stored in connection with your contact inquiries. To exercise these rights, please email Suhaibahmed099@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Privacy Request".
